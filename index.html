What 35 is not

How credit is earned

The desk trades its own capital. After a real usage event, an allowlisted signer posts an on-chain memo:

35-credit:<wallet>:<credit>:<week>

That signed on-chain memo is the sole issuance event. No mint exists until realized desk profit covers rent.
